# Somewhere Between Then and Now

### A Scroll-Driven Life Memoir
*Creative Direction Brief*

---

> A personal art object. Not a portfolio. Not a website.

---

## I. The Vision

This is a scroll-driven, cinematic experience that tells one life story. Not a biography. Not a timeline. A living memory — something you move through rather than read.

The viewer holds a single control: scroll. Scrolling is time. Moving forward moves you through eras of a real life, each with its own visual language, its own sound, its own emotional weather. Time moves only forward. You cannot scroll back, the way you cannot live back.

The life is told as ideas, not documents. There are no photographs, no video, no faces. A handful of plain objects carry the story through an abstract world of light, color, grain, and sound. Someone who did not live this life should recognize its shape anyway.

The piece is built for one person and one purpose: to make something true.

---

> *"The goal is not to explain a life. It is to make someone feel it."*

---

### Guiding Principles

- No narrator. The world speaks.
- No hard cuts. Everything morphs.
- No consistent aesthetic. The visual language changes with the life.
- Only forward. Scroll is time, and time does not run back.
- Abstract, not documentary. Ideas of a life, carried by a few real things.
- The only words are the title. It opens the piece and closes it. Nothing else is written.
- Darkness is shown for what it is — not abstracted, not softened.
- The ending is a horizon. Unfinished. Still walking.

---

## II. The Emotional Arc

The piece has a shape. It begins in warmth and softness, descends into darkness, breaks, rebuilds, descends again, rebuilds again — and ends open. This is not a triumphant arc. It is an honest one.

**Warmth → Fracture → Loss → Identity → Love → Rupture → Reconstruction → Chaos → Arrival → Present → Open**

Each major emotional shift is not signaled with text or UI — it is felt through the changing behavior of the environment itself. The world teaches you how to read it, then changes the rules.

The eras below are the current map of that arc. The arc is fixed. The exact count and borders of the eras are not, and may shift as the piece is built.

---

## III. The Cast

Six objects. Everything else is light, color, grain, particles, and sound. Each object earns its place by returning.

| Object | Its story |
|---|---|
| **The Light** | The first thing. A single warm source that grows from darkness at birth. It stays warm through childhood, goes tungsten and casts the shadows in the abuse era, goes cold in the divorce, multiplies into stadium lights, and is last seen as the dawn on the horizon. The first and last image are the same light. |
| **The Ground** | Formless at birth. Rises as grass in childhood. Becomes a floor, then turf, then a field alone, then dissolves into something digital, then becomes apartment floors, breaks, is redrawn as a foundation, and finally thins toward the horizon. |
| **The Walls** | Appear around the backyard and close it in. Grow wrong in the abuse era — too low, too near, tilted. Empty out in the divorce. Open into sky at college. Carry code as texture in the identity years. Frame windows onto changing cities. Fracture in the breakup. Are drawn again, one line at a time, in Pittsburgh. Thin and dissolve at the end. |
| **The Table** | The dinner table. The last thing to fade when the house empties. It stands alone in the cold. Then it becomes the goal post. |
| **The Lamp** | Carried in during the first apartment. The one constant across three cities and three rooms. It falls in the breakup and does not turn back on. It is never seen again. |
| **The Horizon** | A line. Nothing on either side of it. The piece ends standing in front of it. |

---

## IV. The Viewer

You are walking. The camera moves forward continuously; the world forms ahead of you and dissolves behind. Eras are places you pass through, not slides you are shown.

The walk stops exactly twice: at the dinner table standing in the emptied house, and in the stillness after the breakup. Everywhere else you keep moving, and at the end you are still moving toward the horizon.

In the abuse era the walk goes slightly wrong: the camera tilts, just barely, enough to feel it.

When you stop scrolling, time stops but the world does not. Light breathes, particles drift, sound sustains. The piece is alive while it waits.

Scroll speed feeds the sound. Scroll faster and the audio gains energy — barely in most eras, not at all in the breakup, and completely in the year of chaos, where speed also blurs and oversaturates the image.

## V. The Eras

*Thirteen eras. Each one a world. Each one bleeding into the next.*

---

### 01 — Being Born
*Year Zero*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Warm amber, hazy white, soft gold |
| **Mood** | Pre-language warmth. Pure sensation before memory. |
| **Visual World** | Abstract — light through closed eyelids, formless shapes, no geometry yet. Grain blooms into the frame from nothing. |
| **Audio** | Heartbeat rhythm. Low warm drone. The world is muffled, close. |
| **Transition In** | The piece begins in darkness. The Light grows from the center of the screen. |

---

### 02 — Early Childhood
*The Outside Years*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Grass green, sky blue, golden hour orange |
| **Mood** | Pure joy. Unselfconscious happiness. The world is enormous and safe. |
| **Visual World** | Heavy warm grain, slightly overexposed. Diorama scale — you're inside a small perfect world. Backyard geometry. Open sky. |
| **Audio** | Birds. A screen door. Wind through leaves. The audio feels like a summer you can't quite name. |
| **Transition In** | The formless shapes of birth coalesce into a backyard. The Ground rises as grass from the floor of the screen. |
| **Darkness** | None. This is the before. |

---

### 03 — The Abuse Era
*The House That Changed*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Sickly warm yellows, harsh tungsten orange, deep shadow |
| **Mood** | Fear that lives in the body. The world is the same but wrong. |
| **Visual World** | The warmth corrupts. The grain gets harsh. The Light goes tungsten and casts the shadows — the thing that was comfort becomes the thing that's wrong. The Walls feel unstable: wrong proportions, ceilings too low. Nothing is shown but the room. The room is enough. |
| **Audio** | The birdsong cuts out mid-phrase. A low sub-bass frequency enters. Muffled voices without words. Something that is not quite music. |
| **Transition In** | The backyard shrinks. The Walls appear around it. The sky narrows to a window. |
| **Darkness** | The camera begins to tilt. Just barely. Enough to feel wrong. |

---

### 04 — The Divorce
*The Year of Leaving*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Cold gray-blue, winter white, emptied-out beige |
| **Mood** | Absence. The space where something used to be. |
| **Visual World** | The house empties while the building stays. Things fade in reverse order of intimacy. The Table sits in an empty room. The Walls have moved. Something is missing and you can feel exactly where it was. |
| **Audio** | Record scratch. Silence. Then a single note that doesn't resolve. |
| **Transition In** | The house does not disappear — it empties. The Table remains longest. The walk stops here. |

---

### 05 — Teenage Years & Sports
*The Era of the Body*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Friday night lights gold, turf green, court hardwood brown |
| **Mood** | Identity found in physical excellence. The body as the answer to all questions. Pure belonging. |
| **Visual World** | The cold gives way to the first explosion of color in the piece. Color floods back in — fluid, saturated, no edges. The field is vast and electric. This is the most alive the world has looked. |
| **Audio** | The unresolved note resolves into something that hits. Crowd noise. Cleats on turf. A bass line that builds. |
| **Transition In** | The empty house floor becomes turf. The Table becomes the goal post. The Walls open into sky. |

---

### 06 — College
*The Last Era of the Athlete*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Saturday scarlet, late-night blue, morning gold |
| **Mood** | The peak and the last moment before the fall. Pure freedom with an expiration date you can't see yet. |
| **Visual World** | Saturated and fast. Everything moves. Light, motion blur, the sense of a crowd. The world is enormous and generous. But at the edges things are slightly unfinished. The athlete era is beautiful and borrowed. |
| **Audio** | Layers building — crowd, music, laughter. The most complex sound environment in the piece. Everything on at once. |
| **Transition In** | The field stays but fills with light and stadium sound. The Light multiplies into stadium lights. The sky becomes a stadium ceiling, then opens again. |

---

### 07 — The Identity Years
*After the Athlete*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Muted olive, gray, uncertain browns — searching for a color that fits |
| **Mood** | The athlete died. Something else is trying to be born. Enormous growth inside enormous disorientation. |
| **Visual World** | The stadium empties suddenly. You are left on the field alone. The lights go out one by one. The Ground dissolves into something digital. Code runs across the Walls as texture. The grain is gone; the world is clean and uncertain. The world is literally trying to figure out what it is. |
| **Audio** | The crowd fades to a single ambience — a coffee shop, late at night. Something quieter and more uncertain. The music is searching. |
| **Transition In** | Stadium lights cut one by one. Silence. Then — the hum of a computer. |

---

### 08 — The Seven Years
*Pittsburgh → Nashville → White Plains*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Lavender, muted rose, soft evening light |
| **Mood** | Love and instability living inside each other. Beauty with something unresolved running underneath it always. |
| **Visual World** | Three rooms. Each time the Lamp appears in a new one — the one constant object across three cities. The world is warm and domestic but through the windows the geography keeps changing: one skyline fades into the next into the next. |
| **Audio** | Birds — present throughout. But underneath them, a dark musical current that never fully surfaces. The audio feels like lavender: beautiful and slightly melancholy. |
| **Transition In** | The digital world dissolves into the motion and sound of a move. Then a room. The Lamp is carried in. It turns on. |

---

### 09 — The Breakup
*The Year of Rupture*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Monochrome — all color drained. Near-black and cold white. |
| **Mood** | Loss so total it reorganizes your sense of self. This is the second death in the piece. |
| **Visual World** | The environment literally breaks. The room fragments. The Lamp falls and does not turn back on. Then — stillness. The world goes monochrome and completely still. This is the only moment in the piece where nothing is moving. |
| **Audio** | The birds stop. The dark current surfaces fully — and then it too breaks apart. Record scratch. Then silence that lasts longer than feels comfortable. |
| **Transition In** | The Lamp falls. Everything cracks. Hold on the broken geometry. Then: quiet. The walk stops here. |
| **Darkness** | This is the darkest visual moment in the piece. It should not be softened. Scroll speed does nothing here. |

---

### 10 — Pittsburgh & The House
*The Rebuilding*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Pittsburgh gold and black, interior warm yellows, ownership orange |
| **Mood** | A fresh start with grief still underneath. Pride of arrival. The beginning of something yours. |
| **Visual World** | Color returns slowly — black and gold. From the broken geometry a single line draws itself: the first wall. The Walls rise into a floor plan seen from above, then you're inside it. It's yours. But the monochrome lingers at the edges. |
| **Audio** | The silence breaks with something that feels like a door opening. A new musical thread — quieter than college, more grounded. This is the first music that feels adult. |
| **Transition In** | From the broken geometry, a foundation line draws itself. The Walls rise. |

---

### 11 — The Year of Chaos
*Freedom & Searching*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Neon saturated, city night, the colors of going out |
| **Mood** | Chaotic freedom. Self-discovery through excess. Looking for something in a lot of places. |
| **Visual World** | The house fills with motion — the blur of a year lived at high speed. Color oversaturates. Nothing stays still. It's beautiful and slightly too much. This is the shortest era. It should feel like it went by too fast. |
| **Audio** | Bass-heavy. Fast. Multiple layers fighting for space. Tied to scroll speed more than any other era — scroll fast and it gets louder, more chaotic, more blurred. |
| **Transition In** | The quiet of the new house suddenly fills with light and motion. |

---

### 12 — Finding Her
*The Present Relationship*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Warm earth tones, morning light, something that wants to stay |
| **Mood** | The chaos settles. Something real arrives. Cautious hope. |
| **Visual World** | The oversaturation gradually comes into focus — like adjusting a lens. The world gets quieter and warmer. More considered. A faint grain returns with the warmth. The environment slows and becomes the most present it has been since childhood. |
| **Audio** | The chaotic layers drop away one by one. What's left is something simple — morning sounds, warmth, a melody that feels like it might be okay. |
| **Transition In** | The fast saturated motion slows. Colors warm. The world comes into focus. |

---

### 13 — Somewhere Between Then and Now
*The Present Moment & Open Future*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Uncertain blue-gray, dawn light, unfinished |
| **Mood** | Awe, fear, and excitement living together. New creativity. The story still unfolding. |
| **Visual World** | The environment dissolves at its edges. Not collapse — dissolve, like a painting that isn't finished. The center holds but the periphery fades into abstract geometry. The grain becomes the unfinished edge. Then — the Horizon. A line. Nothing on either side of it. Above it, the Light: the same light from birth, now the dawn. You are still walking toward it. |
| **Audio** | Everything becomes quiet. A single sustained note — not quite a resolution. Something that could go either way. It holds as the screen fades. |
| **Transition In** | The warm world of era 12 opens its edges. The Walls thin. The Horizon appears. |

---

## VI. The Entry and The End

**The entry.** Darkness. The title, and a single word: *begin*. A click or a key starts the heartbeat, and the Light grows. This is the only choice the viewer makes.

**The end.** The Horizon holds. Then it fades to black, and the title returns alone. The sustained note keeps holding under the black and never resolves. Scrolling does nothing now. There is no restart. To see it again, you begin again from darkness.

**Going back.** Scrolling up does nothing. The world does not acknowledge it. A reload starts from darkness.

## VII. Pacing

A full pass at an unhurried pace takes about five minutes. Time is not distributed evenly. Some eras should feel slow, others fast. These weights are the starting point and are tuned by feel.

| Era | Weight | Note |

| 02 Childhood | 1.0 | |
| 03 Abuse | 1.0 | |
| 04 Divorce | 1.2 | Includes the first stop |

## VIII. The Five Major Transitions

*These are the moments the piece lives or dies on. Each one is an emotional event, not just a visual effect. They are choreographed set pieces with their own time. The other seven era boundaries are drifts: palette, grain, sound, and the six objects move gradually to their new values.*

---

### 01 — Parents' Divorce
**Departing:** The family home empties while the building stays. Things fade in reverse order of intimacy — decorations first, furniture last, the Table last of all.

**Arriving:** Cold blue light fills the space where warmth was. The walk stops at the Table.

---

### 02 — Going to College
**Departing:** The house floor becomes turf. The Table becomes the goal post. The Walls open into sky. A record scratch signals the cut.

**Arriving:** Light explodes. Color floods back. This is the most dramatic visual moment in the first half.

---

### 03 — College to First Apartment
**Departing:** The stadium empties in reverse — lights cutting one by one.

**Arriving:** The motion and sound of a move. The Lamp being carried in. It turns on. Lavender warms the room.

---

### 04 — The Breakup
**Departing:** The Lamp falls. The room fragments. Hold on this longer than feels comfortable.

**Arriving:** Monochrome. Stillness. The only moment in the piece where the world stops moving entirely. The walk stops.

---

### 05 — Moving to Pittsburgh / Buying the House
**Departing:** From the broken monochrome geometry, a single line draws itself — the first wall of the house.

**Arriving:** Pittsburgh gold and black. The specific colors of a city that becomes home. The world is yours for the first time.

---

## IX. Sound Design

> *"Audio behaves like a DJ mixing tracks as the user scrolls."*

Each era has its own sound. As the viewer moves between eras, the departing era's sound fades out while the arriving era's fades in. Scroll speed feeds the energy of the mix, with a sensitivity that differs by era.

The sound is generated, not recorded. Drones, heartbeat, wind, the unresolved note, birds, crowd — all are synthesized ideas of those sounds, matching the abstract visual world. A very small number of concrete sounds that resist synthesis (the record scratch, the screen door, cleats, a door opening) may be public-domain recordings. There is no recorded music.

### Key Audio Moments

| Transition | Sound Event |
|---|---|
| Birth → Childhood | Heartbeat fades into birdsong and screen door ambience |
| Childhood → Abuse Era | Birdsong cuts mid-phrase. Sub-bass enters. |
| Divorce | Record scratch. Single unresolved note. Held silence. |
| Sports / College | That note finally resolves — into something that hits. |
| Identity Years | Crowd noise dissolves to coffee shop hum. Searching. |
| The Seven Years | Birds throughout. Dark undercurrent beneath them always. This is lavender. |
| The Breakup | The dark undercurrent surfaces. Breaks. Silence longer than comfortable. |
| Pittsburgh | A door opening. First adult music. Quiet and grounded. |
| Chaos Year | Bass-heavy, scroll-speed reactive, multiple layers competing. |
| Finding Her | Layers dropping away. Something simple and warm. |
| The Horizon | One sustained note. Not resolution. Not despair. A held breath. |

---

## X. Texture

Grain, color grade, vignette, and blur are one continuous thread through the piece, and they trace the life:

- **Birth and childhood** — heavy, warm, slightly overexposed. Home-movie grain.
- **Abuse** — the same grain gone harsh.
- **Divorce** — fine, cold.
- **Identity through chaos** — gone. Clean, digital.
- **Finding her** — a faint return with the warmth.
- **The horizon** — grain becomes the unfinished edge of the painting.

---

## XI. Materials

Nothing in the piece is a document of the life. There are no photographs, no video, no recorded music. Everything is made: the six objects, the worlds around them, the sound. The only sourced material is a handful of public-domain sound recordings for moments synthesis can't carry.

---

## XII. The North Star

---

> *"This is a personal art object. It is for one person. It should be true before it is beautiful, dark where it was dark, warm where it was warm, and end on a horizon because the story is not finished."*

---

The measure of success is not technical. It is whether someone who did not live this life feels, for a moment, that they did.
