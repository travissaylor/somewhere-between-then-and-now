# Somewhere Between Then and Now

### A Scroll-Driven Life Memoir
*Creative Direction & Technical Brief*

---

> A personal art object. Not a portfolio. Not a website.

---

## I. The Vision

This is a scroll-driven, cinematic web experience that tells one life story. Not a biography. Not a timeline. A living memory — something you move through rather than read.

The user holds a single control: scroll. Scrolling is time. Moving forward moves you through eras of a real life, each with its own visual language, its own sound, its own emotional weather.

The piece is built for one person and one purpose: to make something true.

---

> *"The goal is not to explain a life. It is to make someone feel it."*

---

### Guiding Principles

- No narrator. The world speaks.
- No hard cuts. Everything morphs.
- No consistent aesthetic. The visual language changes with the life.
- Darkness is shown for what it is — not abstracted, not softened.
- The ending is a horizon. Unfinished. Still walking.

---

## II. The Emotional Arc

The piece has a shape. It begins in warmth and softness, descends into darkness, breaks, rebuilds, descends again, rebuilds again — and ends open. This is not a triumphant arc. It is an honest one.

**Warmth → Fracture → Loss → Identity → Love → Rupture → Reconstruction → Chaos → Arrival → Present → Open**

Each major emotional shift is not signaled with text or UI — it is felt through the changing behavior of the environment itself. The world teaches you how to read it, then changes the rules.

---

## III. The Eras

*Thirteen eras. Each one a world. Each one bleeding into the next.*

---

### 01 — Being Born
*Year Zero*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Warm amber, hazy white, soft gold |
| **Mood** | Pre-language warmth. Pure sensation before memory. |
| **Visual World** | Abstract — light through closed eyelids, formless shapes, no geometry yet. Super 8 grain blooms into the frame from nothing. |
| **Audio** | Heartbeat rhythm. Low warm drone. The world is muffled, close. |
| **Transition In** | The piece begins in darkness. A single warm light source grows from the center of the screen. |

---

### 02 — Early Childhood
*The Outside Years*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Grass green, sky blue, golden hour orange |
| **Mood** | Pure joy. Unselfconscious happiness. The world is enormous and safe. |
| **Visual World** | Super 8 film grain. Warm and slightly overexposed. Diorama scale — you're inside a small perfect world. Backyard geometry. Open sky. |
| **Audio** | Birds. Screen door slamming. Wind through leaves. The audio feels like a summer you can't quite name. |
| **Transition In** | The abstract shapes from birth coalesce into a backyard. Grass rises from the floor of the screen. |
| **Darkness** | None. This is the before. |

---

### 03 — The Abuse Era
*The House That Changed*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Sickly warm yellows, harsh tungsten orange, deep shadow |
| **Mood** | Fear that lives in the body. The world is the same but wrong. |
| **Visual World** | The Super 8 warmth corrupts. The grain gets harsh. Shadows deepen beyond what's natural. The geometry of rooms begins to feel unstable — slightly wrong proportions, ceilings too low. |
| **Audio** | The birdsong cuts out mid-phrase. A low sub-bass frequency enters. Muffled voices. Something that is not quite music. |
| **Transition In** | The backyard shrinks. Walls appear around it. The sky narrows to a window. |
| **Darkness** | The camera begins to tilt. Just barely. Enough to feel wrong. |

---

### 04 — The Divorce
*The Year of Leaving*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Cold gray-blue, winter white, emptied-out beige |
| **Mood** | Absence. The space where something used to be. |
| **Visual World** | Objects get left behind as the world rebuilds around their absence. A dinner table sits in an empty room. The walls have moved. Something is missing and you can feel exactly where it was. |
| **Audio** | Record scratch transition. Silence. Then a single note that doesn't resolve. |
| **Transition In** | The house does not disappear — it empties. Furniture fades. The table remains longest. |

---

### 05 — Teenage Years & Sports
*The Era of the Body*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Friday night lights gold, turf green, court hardwood brown |
| **Mood** | Identity found in physical excellence. The body as the answer to all questions. Pure belonging. |
| **Visual World** | The cold of divorce gives way to the first explosion of color in the piece. Ink floods back in — fluid, saturated, no edges. The football field is vast and electric. This is the most alive the world has looked. |
| **Audio** | The record scratch resolves into something that hits. Crowd noise. The specific sound of cleats on turf. A bass line that builds. |
| **Transition In** | The empty house floor transforms into a football field. The table becomes a goal post. |

---

### 06 — College
*The Last Era of the Athlete*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Saturday scarlet, late-night blue, morning gold |
| **Mood** | The peak and the last moment before the fall. Pure freedom with an expiration date you can't see yet. |
| **Visual World** | Saturated and fast. Everything moves. Crowds, light, motion blur. The world is enormous and generous. But if you look closely at the edges — things are slightly unfinished. The athlete era is beautiful and borrowed. |
| **Audio** | Layered audio stems building — crowd, music, laughter. The most complex sound environment in the piece. Everything on at once. |
| **Transition In** | The football field stays but fills with bodies, light, stadium sound. The sky becomes a stadium ceiling, then opens again. |

---

### 07 — The Identity Years
*After the Athlete*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Muted olive, gray, uncertain browns — searching for a color that fits |
| **Mood** | The athlete died. Something else is trying to be born. Enormous growth inside enormous disorientation. |
| **Visual World** | The stadium empties suddenly. You are left on the field alone. The lights go out one by one. The grass starts to dissolve into something urban, digital. Code fragments. IDE windows. The world is literally trying to figure out what it is. |
| **Audio** | The crowd noise fades to a single ambience — a coffee shop, late at night. Something quieter and more uncertain. The music is searching. |
| **Transition In** | Stadium lights cut one by one. Silence. Then — the hum of a computer. |

---

### 08 — The Seven Years
*Pittsburgh → Nashville → White Plains*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Lavender, muted rose, soft evening light |
| **Mood** | Love and instability living inside each other. Beauty with something unresolved running underneath it always. |
| **Visual World** | Three apartments. Each time the same lamp appears in a new room — the one constant object across all three cities. The world is warm and domestic but if you look out the windows, the geography keeps changing. Pittsburgh skyline fades into Nashville fades into White Plains. |
| **Audio** | Birds chirping — present throughout. But underneath them, a dark musical current that never fully surfaces. The audio feels like lavender: beautiful and slightly melancholy. |
| **Transition In** | The code environment dissolves into a moving truck. Then an apartment. The lamp turns on. |

---

### 09 — The Breakup
*The Year of Rupture*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Monochrome — all color drained. Near-black and cold white. |
| **Mood** | Loss so total it reorganizes your sense of self. This is the second death in the piece. |
| **Visual World** | The environment literally breaks. The apartment geometry fragments. The lamp falls and does not turn back on. Then — stillness. The world goes monochrome and completely still. This is the only moment in the piece where nothing is moving. |
| **Audio** | The birds stop. The dark musical current that was running under the seven years surfaces fully — and then it too breaks apart. Record scratch. Then silence that lasts longer than feels comfortable. |
| **Transition In** | The lamp falls. Everything cracks. Hold on the broken geometry. Then: quiet. |
| **Darkness** | This is the darkest visual moment in the piece. It should not be softened. |

---

### 10 — Pittsburgh & The House
*The Rebuilding*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Pittsburgh gold and black, interior warm yellows, ownership orange |
| **Mood** | A fresh start with grief still underneath. Pride of arrival. The beginning of something yours. |
| **Visual World** | Color returns slowly — Pittsburgh's specific palette, black and gold. The broken geometry of the breakup rebuilds itself into a house floor plan, seen from above, then you're inside it. It's yours. The world is yours. But the monochrome breakup lingers at the edges. |
| **Audio** | The silence breaks with something that feels like a door opening. A new musical thread — quieter than college, more grounded. This is the first music that feels adult. |
| **Transition In** | From the broken geometry of the breakup, a foundation line draws itself. Walls rise. |

---

### 11 — The Year of Chaos
*Freedom & Searching*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Neon saturated, city night, the colors of going out |
| **Mood** | Chaotic freedom. Self-discovery through excess. Looking for something in a lot of places. |
| **Visual World** | The house fills with motion — parties, people, the blur of a year lived at high speed. Color oversaturates. Things move fast. Nothing stays still. It's beautiful and slightly too much. |
| **Audio** | Bass-heavy. Fast. Multiple audio stems fighting for space. Beat-matched to scroll speed more than any other era — scroll fast and it gets louder and more chaotic. |
| **Transition In** | The quiet of the new house suddenly fills with light and motion. |

---

### 12 — Finding Her
*The Present Relationship*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Warm earth tones, morning light, something that wants to stay |
| **Mood** | The chaos settles. Something real arrives. Cautious hope. |
| **Visual World** | The oversaturated chaos gradually comes into focus — like adjusting a lens. The world gets quieter and warmer. More considered. The environment slows down and becomes the most present it has been since childhood. |
| **Audio** | The chaotic stems drop away one by one. What's left is something simple — morning sounds, warmth, a melody that feels like it might be okay. |
| **Transition In** | The fast saturated motion slows. Colors warm. The world comes into focus. |

---

### 13 — Somewhere Between Then and Now
*The Present Moment & Open Future*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Uncertain blue-gray, dawn light, unfinished |
| **Mood** | Awe, fear, and excitement living together. The AI age. New creativity. The story still unfolding. |
| **Visual World** | The environment begins to dissolve at its edges. Not collapse — dissolve. Like a painting that isn't finished. The center holds but the periphery fades into abstract geometry. Code and creativity coexist in the visual space. Then — a horizon. Just a horizon line. Nothing on either side of it. You are standing in front of it. The piece ends here. |
| **Audio** | Everything becomes quiet. A single sustained note — not quite a resolution. Something that could go either way. It holds as the screen fades. |
| **Transition In** | The warm world of era 12 opens its edges. The walls thin. The horizon appears. |

## IV. The Five Major Transitions

*These are the moments the piece lives or dies on. Each one is an emotional event, not just a visual effect.*

---

### 01 — Parents' Divorce
**Departing:** The family home empties while the building stays. Objects fade in reverse order of intimacy — decorations first, furniture last, the dinner table last of all.

**Arriving:** Cold blue light fills the space where warmth was.

---

### 02 — Going to College
**Departing:** The house floor transforms into turf. The ceiling opens into sky. A record scratch signals the cut.

**Arriving:** Light explodes. Color floods back. This is the most dramatic visual moment in the first half.

---

### 03 — College to First Apartment
**Departing:** The stadium empties in reverse — lights cutting one by one.

**Arriving:** A moving truck. A lamp being carried in. It turns on. Lavender warms the room.

---

### 04 — The Breakup
**Departing:** The lamp falls. The geometry of the apartment fragments. Hold on this longer than feels comfortable.

**Arriving:** Monochrome. Stillness. The only moment in the piece where the world stops moving entirely.

---

### 05 — Moving to Pittsburgh / Buying the House
**Departing:** From the broken monochrome geometry, a single line draws itself — the first wall of the house.

**Arriving:** Pittsburgh gold and black. The specific colors of a city that becomes home. The world is yours for the first time.

## V. Sound Design

> *"Audio behaves like a DJ mixing tracks as the user scrolls."*

Each era has its own audio stem or stems. As the user scrolls between eras, the audio crossfades — stems from the departing era fade out while the arriving era's stems fade in. Scroll speed affects playback energy.

### Key Audio Moments

| Transition | Sound Event |
|---|---|
| Birth → Childhood | Heartbeat fades into birdsong and screen door ambience |
| Childhood → Abuse Era | Birdsong cuts mid-phrase. Sub-bass enters. |
| Divorce | Record scratch. Single unresolved note. Held silence. |
| Sports / College | That note finally resolves — into something that hits. |
| Identity Years | Crowd noise dissolves to coffee shop hum. Searching. |
| The Seven Years | Birds throughout. Dark undercurrent beneath them always. This is lavender. |
| The Breakup | The dark undercurrent surfaces. Breaks. Silence longer than comfortable. |
| Pittsburgh | A door opening. First adult music. Quiet and grounded. |
| Chaos Year | Bass-heavy, scroll-speed reactive, multiple stems competing. |
| Finding Her | Stems dropping away. Something simple and warm. |
| The Horizon | One sustained note. Not resolution. Not despair. A held breath. |

## VI. Technical Architecture

### Recommended Stack

| Layer | Tool |
|---|---|
| **Scroll Engine** | GSAP ScrollTrigger — maps scroll position to timeline progress |
| **3D / Visuals** | React Three Fiber — each era is a Three.js scene |
| **Shader Transitions** | WebGL GLSL — morphing geometries, color shifts, film grain, dissolves |
| **Audio Engine** | Tone.js or Web Audio API — crossfading stems, scroll-reactive mixing |
| **Framework** | Next.js — routing, asset optimization, Vercel deployment |
| **Asset Pipeline** | ffmpeg for video, Sharp for images, custom GLSL for photo-to-3D |

### Core Technical Challenges

**Scene-to-scene shader morphing** — each transition is a custom GLSL shader that interpolates between two scene states. This is the hardest and most important technical problem.

**Scroll normalization** — GSAP ScrollTrigger must map scroll distance to emotional pacing, not equal time intervals. Some eras should feel slow, others fast.

**Audio stem management** — preloading, crossfading, and scroll-reactive mixing across 13 audio zones without jank or latency.

**Real photo integration** — actual photographs need to blend seamlessly into 3D environments, treated with matching film grain and color grading.

### Build Phases

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | Static scroll scaffold — GSAP timeline, era zones, color transitions only. No 3D yet. |
| **Phase 2** | Audio system — stem loading, crossfading, scroll reactivity. |
| **Phase 3** | 3D environments per era — placeholder geometry, establish visual language. |
| **Phase 4** | Shader transitions between eras — the hardest phase. |
| **Phase 5** | Photo and video integration into 3D scenes. |
| **Phase 6** | Polish — grain, color grading, audio mixing, pacing. |
